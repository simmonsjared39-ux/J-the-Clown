Memories And Beyond

The story follows Dr. Eggman’s latest scheme: upgrading Metal Sonic into the ultimate fighting machine by stealing Sonic’s life data and harnessing the power of the Chaos Emeralds. This new, enhanced Metal Sonic becomes a far more dangerous opponent than before, capable of outmatching Sonic and his friends in raw powe.Faced with overwhelming odds, Sonic and his allies — Amy Rose, Knuckles the Echidna, Tails, Shadow, Rouge the Bat, and Dr. Eggman — realize that victory won’t come from speed or brute force alone. Instead, they must rely on the friendships and bonds they’ve built through their many adventures.
